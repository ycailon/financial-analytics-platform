# Report page plan

## 1. Executive Overview

Decision: Which companies are growing profitably, and how has that changed?

- Company and fiscal-year slicers
- Revenue card
- Net Income card
- Revenue YoY card
- Profit Margin card
- Revenue and Net Income trend
- Profit Margin by company
- Revenue growth vs Profit Margin scatter

## 2. Company Comparison

Decision: How do companies compare on scale, profitability and balance-sheet position?

- Revenue by company
- Net Income by company
- Operating Margin by company
- ROA / ROE comparison
- Debt-to-Assets comparison
- Detail matrix by fiscal year

## 3. Economic Context

Decision: What did inflation and labor-market conditions look like during the same periods?

- CPI trend
- Inflation YoY trend
- Unemployment trend
- Company revenue-growth trend beside macro indicators
- Annotation explaining that the comparison is contextual, not causal

## 4. Data Quality & Lineage

Decision: Can I trust and trace the numbers?

- Rows loaded by company and metric
- Missing metric/year coverage
- Source concept table
- Filing date and accession-number detail
- Quality status summary
